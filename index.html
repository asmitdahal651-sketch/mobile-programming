Paper was never going to last this long

A fridge is meant to run for a decade. The receipt that proves you own it is lucky to survive a monsoon.

Bills fade or get lost

Thermal-printed receipts fade within months. Warranty cards get misplaced in a move — often just when the product breaks and you need them most.

Warranties go untracked

Nobody remembers a coverage window written in fine print two years ago — until the repair bill lands and the warranty has already lapsed.

Second-hand is a guessing game

Buying a used laptop or appliance in Kathmandu usually means trusting the seller's word on age and repairs, with no way to check any of it.

From receipt to record, in one scan

One flow, from the day you buy something to the day you pass it on.

  1. 01

    Store the bill

    Photograph or upload the purchase bill and warranty card right after you buy. MeroBill reads the product, date and warranty terms automatically.

  2. 02

    Get a QR seal for the product

    A printable QR seal links the physical item to its digital record, so anyone who scans it lands on the verified history — not just your say-so.

  3. 03

    Track warranty, get reminded

    MeroBill watches the expiry date and nudges you before coverage runs out, with enough time to actually use it.

  4. 04

    Log every repair

    Each service visit — date, technician, what was fixed — gets added to the same record, building a servicing history instead of a pile of loose slips.

  5. 05

    Transfer ownership when you sell

    Selling the product hands the entire record — bill, warranty, repairs — to the new owner in one confirmed transfer. No re-typing, no lost history.

Buying second-hand? Scan before you pay.

Instead of taking a seller's word for it, scan the product's QR seal and see its actual paper trail — purchase date, warranty status, repair count and how many hands it's passed through.
