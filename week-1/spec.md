MeroBill – Digital Product Ownership and Warranty Wallet

Problem Statement
Consumers in Kathmandu often rely on physical bills and warranty cards when purchasing expensive products such as smartphones, laptops, and home appliances. These documents can easily be lost, damaged, or faded, making it difficult to prove a purchase or claim a warranty. Customers may also forget warranty expiry dates or lose important product information.
The problem becomes more significant when products are sold as second-hand items. Buyers often have no reliable way to verify the product's original purchase, warranty status, repair history, or ownership. They usually have to depend on the seller's claims, which can create uncertainty and increase the risk of purchasing products with false or incomplete information. Therefore, there is a need for a digital system that securely stores product records and allows important product information to remain accessible even when ownership changes.

Objectives
The main objective of MeroBill is to develop a digital product passport system that securely stores and manages product purchase, warranty, repair, and ownership information.
The specific objectives are to:
1) Digitize and securely store product bills and purchase records.
2) Allow users to track product warranties and receive expiry reminders.
3) Use QR codes to connect physical products with their digital records.
4) Maintain a history of product repairs and servicing.
5) Enable secure digital transfer of product ownership.
6) Allow buyers to verify a product's purchase, warranty, and ownership information before purchasing second-hand products.
7) Reduce dependency on physical bills and warranty cards.
